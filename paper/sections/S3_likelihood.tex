\section{Likelihood derivatives and LAN reduction}

Write
\[
 R_{k,j}(\theta)=Y_{k,j+1}-\rho_{k,n}(\theta)Y_{k,j},
 \qquad
 v_{k,n}(\theta,\sigma)=\sigma^2q_k^2h_{k,n}(\theta).
\]
The score contribution is
\begin{align*}
 \partial_\theta\ell_{k,j}
 &=\frac{\dot\rho_{k,n}Y_{k,j}R_{k,j}}{v_{k,n}}
 +\frac12\left(\frac{R_{k,j}^2}{v_{k,n}}-1\right)
 \partial_\theta\log h_{k,n},\\
 \partial_\sigma\ell_{k,j}
 &=\frac1\sigma\left(\frac{R_{k,j}^2}{v_{k,n}}-1\right),
\end{align*}
where
\[
 \dot\rho_{k,n}=-\lambda_k\Delta_n\rho_{k,n}.
\]
Conditional Gaussian moments give the exact information. Uniform expansions under $\lambda_N\Delta_n\to0$ show
\[
 \Lambda_N^{-1}I_{\theta\theta}\to\frac{T}{2\theta_0},
 \qquad
 (Nn)^{-1}I_{\sigma\sigma}\to\frac{2}{\sigma_0^2},
 \qquad
 (\Lambda_NNn)^{-1/2}I_{\theta\sigma}\to0.
\]

The central sequence is a sum of independent modal martingale blocks. For the drift coordinate, fourth moments are bounded by a constant multiple of $\lambda_k^2$; hence
\[
 \frac{\sum_{k\le N}\lambda_k^2}{\Lambda_N^2}
 \le\frac{\lambda_N}{\Lambda_N}\to0.
\]
For the scale coordinate the corresponding ratio is $O(N^{-1})$. These bounds give Lindeberg and score CLTs.

A third-order Taylor expansion of the exact Gaussian likelihood, uniform on compact local parameter sets, produces the LAN remainder. Global consistency follows from Kullback--Leibler separation of transition means and variances. The score equation and Hessian convergence yield the asymptotic linear representation. A polynomial-type likelihood-ratio inequality, obtained from uniform score moments, local curvature, and compact global separation, gives uniform integrability and moment convergence in the sense of \citet{Yoshida2011}.
