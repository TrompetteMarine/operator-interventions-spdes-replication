\closuredraft
\begin{proof}
Write $Z_{N,n}:=\an(\widehat\Delta-\Delta)$, so that
$\an\widehat\Delta=\an\Delta+Z_{N,n}$ and, by
Corollary~\ref{cor:effect}, $Z_{N,n}\Rightarrow\mathbb G_\Delta$ in
$C(\cT_0)$; in particular $\norm{Z_{N,n}}_\infty=O_{\Pp}(1)$ and the limit
process has uniformly continuous sample paths on the compact $\cT_0$.

(i) If $\sup_{\cT_0}\Delta=:-c_0<0$, then
$\sup_{\cT_0}\an\widehat\Delta\le-\an c_0+O_{\Pp}(1)\to-\infty$, so
$S^{\le}_{N,n}=\sup_{\cT_0}[\an\widehat\Delta]_+=0$ with probability tending
to one, and in particular $S^{\le}_{N,n}\to0$ in probability.

(ii) Let $\cC_0\ne\varnothing$ and assume the local separation condition:
for every $\eta>0$ there is $c(\eta)>0$ with
$\Delta(\tau)\le-c(\eta)$ whenever $d(\tau,\cC_0)\ge\eta$. Fix $\eta>0$ and
split $\cT_0=\cC_0^\eta\cup(\cT_0\setminus\cC_0^\eta)$,
$\cC_0^\eta:=\{\tau:d(\tau,\cC_0)<\eta\}$.
On the complement, $\an\widehat\Delta\le-\an c(\eta)+\norm{Z_{N,n}}_\infty$,
whose positive part vanishes with probability tending to one; hence
\[
 S^{\le}_{N,n}
 =\sup_{\cC_0^\eta}\big[\an\Delta+Z_{N,n}\big]_+
 +o_{\Pp}(1)
 \le\sup_{\cC_0^\eta}\big[Z_{N,n}\big]_+ +o_{\Pp}(1),
\]
using $\Delta\le0$ on $\cT_0$; and, restricting the supremum to
$\cC_0\subseteq\cC_0^\eta$ where $\Delta=0$,
$S^{\le}_{N,n}\ge\sup_{\cC_0}[Z_{N,n}]_+$.
The map $z\mapsto\sup_{A}[z]_+$ is continuous on $C(\cT_0)$ for compact
$A$, so the continuous mapping theorem gives
\[
 \sup_{\cC_0}[Z_{N,n}]_+\Rightarrow\sup_{\cC_0}[\mathbb G_\Delta]_+,
 \qquad
 \sup_{\cC_0^\eta}[Z_{N,n}]_+\Rightarrow\sup_{\cC_0^\eta}[\mathbb G_\Delta]_+ .
\]
Finally, $\sup_{\cC_0^\eta}[\mathbb G_\Delta]_+
\downarrow\sup_{\cC_0}[\mathbb G_\Delta]_+$ almost surely as
$\eta\downarrow0$, by uniform continuity of $\mathbb G_\Delta$ and
compactness ($\cC_0^\eta\downarrow\cC_0$). A standard sandwich
(portmanteau along $\eta\downarrow0$) then yields
$S^{\le}_{N,n}\Rightarrow\sup_{\cC_0}[\mathbb G_\Delta]_+$, as claimed;
the argument is the compact-index case of the directional delta method for
supremum functionals \citep[cf.][]{Carcamo2020,FangSantos2019}, proved here
directly to keep the chain self-contained. The
same argument applies verbatim to tail- and quantile-effect surfaces with
their loadings.
\end{proof}
