\closuredraft
\begin{proof}
Write $\widehat G_s(\tau):=r_{N,n}\widehat G(\tau)/\widehat s_\kappa(\tau)$
for the studentised surface. Under the expansions in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:st-band},
$r_{N,n}(\widehat G-G_0)=d^\top H_{N,n}+R_{N,n}$ with
$\sup_\tau|R_{N,n}|/\widehat s_\kappa=o_{\Pp}(1)$ and
$\sup_\tau|d^\top H_{N,n}|/\widehat s_\kappa=O_{\Pp}(1)$.

(i) \emph{Inclusion.} For $\tau\in\cC_0$, $G_0(\tau)=0$, so
$\widehat G_s(\tau)=d(\tau)^\top H_{N,n}/\widehat s_\kappa(\tau)
+o_{\Pp}(1)$, whose supremum over $\cC_0$ is $O_{\Pp}(1)$ by the global
Cauchy--Schwarz bound. Since $\eta_{N,n}\to\infty$,
$\Pp\big(\inf_{\cC_0}\widehat G_s\ge-\eta_{N,n}\big)\to1$, i.e.\
$\cC_0\subseteq\widehat\cC_{N,n}$ wp$\to$1.

(ii) \emph{No over-inclusion.} Let $\cU\supseteq\cC_0$ be open in $\cT_0$;
by the separation assumption there is $c(\cU)>0$ with
$G_0\le-c(\cU)$ off $\cU$. Since $\norm{\widehat s_\kappa}_\infty$ is
bounded and bounded away from zero in probability,
\[
 \sup_{\tau\notin\cU}\widehat G_s(\tau)
 \;\le\;-\frac{r_{N,n}\,c(\cU)}{\norm{\widehat s_\kappa}_\infty}
 +\sup_\tau\frac{|d(\tau)^\top H_{N,n}|+|R_{N,n}(\tau)|}
 {\widehat s_\kappa(\tau)}
 \;\le\;-c'\,r_{N,n}\,\big(1+o_{\Pp}(1)\big)+O_{\Pp}(1)
\]
for some $c'>0$, using the global Cauchy--Schwarz bound and Step 2 of the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:st-band}. Since $\eta_{N,n}/r_{N,n}\to0$, these indices satisfy
$\widehat G_s<-\eta_{N,n}$ wp$\to$1, so
$\widehat\cC_{N,n}\subseteq\cU$ wp$\to$1.

(iii)--(iv) \emph{Size.} On the null, decompose the statistic over
$\widehat\cC_{N,n}$: by (i)--(ii), for every open $\cU\supseteq\cC_0$,
\[
 \sup_{\cC_0}\big[\widehat G_s\big]_+\le
 S^\le_{N,n}\le\sup_{\cU\cap\{\text{indices kept}\}}\big[\widehat
 G_s\big]_+ +o_{\Pp}(1)
 \le\sup_{\cU}\big[d^\top H_{N,n}/\widehat s_\kappa\big]_+ +o_{\Pp}(1),
\]
using $G_0\le0$ throughout the null. The bootstrap statistic
\eqref{eq:one-bootstrap} is the same functional of $H^*$ over the estimated
set. By (i)--(ii) the estimated set is sandwiched between $\cC_0$ and any
fixed $\cU$, wp$\to$1; conditional convergence of $H^*$ and the shrinking
argument of the WP4 contact-set closure (letting $\cU\downarrow\cC_0$ along
the a.s.-continuity of the limiting Gaussian functional) give that the
conditional critical value converges to the $(1-\alpha)$ quantile of
$\sup_{\cC_0}[d^\top H/s_\kappa]_+$, majorising the limit of the statistic;
hence asymptotic size $\le\alpha$, with equality at regular boundary nulls
where the limiting distribution is continuous and strictly increasing at its
quantile.

(v) \emph{Consistency.} If $\sup_{\cT_0}G_0=:c_1>0$, pick $\tau_1$ with
$G_0(\tau_1)\ge c_1/2$; then
$S^\le_{N,n}\ge\widehat G_s(\tau_1)
\ge c\,r_{N,n}(1+o_{\Pp}(1))\to\infty$, while the critical value is
$O_{\Pp}(1)$ (a positive-part supremum of a studentised Gaussian over a set
of indices, bounded by the global Cauchy--Schwarz bound). Rejection
probability tends to one.
\end{proof}
